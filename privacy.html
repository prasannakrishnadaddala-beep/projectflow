Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what VEWIT collects, how we use it, how we secure it, and what choices customers and end users have when using the VEWIT platform.

HomeTerms of ServiceSecurity

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide VEWIT’s workspace, project management, messaging, ticketing, analytics, and AI-assisted collaboration features.

  • Account data: name, email address, role, profile details, authentication settings.
  • Workspace data: projects, tasks, tickets, comments, files, messages, teams, reports, timelines, reminders, and settings.
  • Technical data: device/browser details, IP address, session metadata, log records, error telemetry, and security events.
  • Billing and commercial data: plan details, invoices, payment references, and workspace subscription history.
  • AI configuration data: customer-provided AI API keys and prompts submitted through VEWIT AI features.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and maintain customer workspaces and user accounts.
  • To authenticate users, enforce permissions, and protect workspace isolation.
  • To power collaboration features such as tasks, tickets, messaging, notifications, and analytics.
  • To generate AI-assisted outputs when users invoke VEWIT AI features.
  • To improve product reliability, support customers, investigate abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

3. AI and Workspace Data

When a workspace enables VEWIT AI, the system may use relevant workspace context such as projects, tasks, users, deadlines, and task history to answer prompts or perform requested actions. Customers are responsible for reviewing AI-generated output before acting on it. Customer-supplied AI keys are used only to provide enabled AI functionality for that workspace.

Important

AI functionality can be disabled at the workspace level. Other core workspace features continue to operate without AI enabled.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal data. We may share information only in limited circumstances:

  • With infrastructure, email, hosting, analytics, security, or payment processors acting on our behalf.
  • With AI model providers only when a workspace explicitly uses AI features.
  • When required by law, regulation, court order, or to protect rights, users, systems, or the public.
  • As part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or asset sale subject to appropriate confidentiality protections.

5. Data Retention

We retain customer data for as long as needed to provide the service, satisfy legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention may vary by data type. After termination, data may be retained for a limited period for backup, recovery, security review, and compliance purposes before deletion.

6. Security Measures

VEWIT uses layered technical and organizational safeguards including authentication controls, role-based access, encrypted transport, session protection, password hashing, logging, and tenant-aware data access patterns. No security program can guarantee absolute protection, and customers remain responsible for account hygiene, device security, and access management.

7. Your Choices

  • Workspace admins can manage team access, roles, two-factor authentication, and workspace settings.
  • Users may update profile information and authentication methods where available.
  • Customers may request data export, correction, or deletion support subject to law and contract.

8. International Processing

Customer data may be processed in jurisdictions where VEWIT or its subprocessors operate. Where required, we use appropriate contractual, technical, and organizational safeguards for cross-border processing.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes may be posted through the product, website, or direct customer communication. Continued use of VEWIT after an update means the updated policy applies.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact privacy@vewit.in or support@vewit.in.
